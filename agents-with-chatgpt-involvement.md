# ChatGPT involvement (advice, not rules)
Written 2026-11-16 by the involvement setup. Edit freely. Nothing here overrides the
workflow rules in the Skill; if they conflict, the Skill wins.

## Use ChatGPT for
- Send substantial planning, difficult read-only reviews, and hard investigations to ChatGPT.

## Do not use ChatGPT for
- Keep routine implementation and small tasks with Codex.

## Before calling, also give ChatGPT
- A concise complete summary: goal, constraints, relevant paths, decisions, and the specific question.

## What to ask back
- Match the result to the task: implementation plans for planning, findings for reviews, and usable drafts for prose.

## Sessions
- Use one ChatGPT chat per task. Read-only reviews use a fresh temporary chat as required by the Skill.
